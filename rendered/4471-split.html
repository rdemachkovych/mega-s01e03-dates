The three problems

parser — day and month swapped. The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so 7 April comes out as 4 July. Every channel, every day-first tenant. “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” This is the bug #4471 describes, and the fix that is queued.

hint — the booking-v3 form tells people to type month first. Tenants on booking-v3 show MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Their people type what the hint says; the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. It reads the same as parser in a report, but it only comes through the form, only for booking-v3 tenants, and the parser fix does not touch it: the form hint has to change.

one-day — the day moves by one, the month stays. “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” No day/month swap can do that. It points at time zones (an invite rendered in the viewer’s zone, a date stored as midnight UTC). The desk already read these reports this way (oneDayMove) and still filed them under the parser.

Why it was merged

Subjects. Decision 0001 merges on the subject line, and the three problems share the same subjects: “Date is wrong”, “Wrong day on the invite”, even “Month and day swapped?” come in for each of them. The subject says the date is wrong; the body and the tenant say how. Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the tenant on dozens of these reports, so it can’t split them either.

For the desk

  • Triage a date report by tenant and channel first, then the body: day/month swapped through a booking-v3 form is hint; one day off is one-day; any other swap is parser.
  • Decision 0001 stays as it is (decisions are not edited). Splitting on subject alone hid problems since April; a new record should replace it.
